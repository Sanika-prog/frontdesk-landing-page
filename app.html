Patient Assistant — Chat Widget

This is a working simulation of the embeddable website chat widget described in the team's Design and Technology Plan. Try booking an appointment, or tap "Talk to a person" at any time.

Frontdesk Escalated
Online — Suburban GP Clinic

1 · Starting point

Quick-action chips remove the "where do I start" step. A patient can also type freely at any point.

2 · Live availability

Time slots are simulated as pulled directly from the clinic's booking system, matching the team's real-time sync design.

3 · Confirmation + preparation prompt

My individual feature: after booking, Frontdesk shows short, clinic-supplied preparation notes — reducing day-of delays without extra reception work.

Always-available human handoff

"Talk to a person" is visible at every step, bypassing classification entirely and handing the full conversation to reception. This also writes live to the Practice Manager Dashboard's queue and activity log — open dashboard.html after escalating to see it appear.

My individual feature: language selector

Given Australia's culturally diverse patient base (flagged as a growth feature in the team's plan), I've added a visible language switcher. In production this would hand off to the conversational AI engine to respond fluently in-language; this preview demonstrates the UI hook.
